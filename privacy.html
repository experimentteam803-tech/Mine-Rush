Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use MineRush - Idle Energy Simulator (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account to save your in-game simulator progress, we collect your email address and basic account profile details managed securely via Firebase Authentication.

2. Use of Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide and maintain game simulator services and save level progress.
  • Deliver in-game achievements, streaks, and virtual game rewards.
  • Maintain user account security.

3. In-Game Virtual Items Disclaimer

MineRush is an idle simulation game. All points, coins, energy cores, and gems within the application are strictly virtual in-game items for game progression purposes and hold no real-world monetary or financial value.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app may integrate third-party ad networks (such as Start.io) to deliver sponsored video ads for optional in-game bonuses. These partners may collect device identifiers and standard analytical data to display relevant advertisements in accordance with their privacy policies.

5. Data Security & Deletion

Your data is stored securely using cloud infrastructure. You can request account deletion or data removal by contacting us via our support channel.
