MineRush Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MineRush ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Data Collection

We collect email address for account authentication and user-initiated cloud mining logs. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising ID (GAID), and IP addresses to serve personalized or contextual advertisements in compliance with Google Play Policies.

3. Security

User account data is protected by Firebase enterprise cloud encryption standards.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, contact us via support in the app.
